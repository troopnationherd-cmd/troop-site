About the Artist

The Sound of Brandon Shephard

Zebra Troop is the indie psychedelic soul project of Brandon Shephard, a singular artist blending the warmth of classic R&B, the groove of 70s soul, and modern indie sensibilities into something entirely his own.

Troop Nation is the fan-run community dedicated to celebrating his music, sharing his art with the world, and connecting listeners who feel that same pull to his sound. This is home for the Herd.
